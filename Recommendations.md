# Swiggy Restaurant Portfolio & Market Intelligence: Business Recommendations


Executive Summary
This document outlines data-driven strategic recommendations derived from exploratory data analysis of the Swiggy restaurant dataset (spanning 8,600+ listings across major metropolitan markets). The analysis evaluates operational efficiencies, pricing thresholds, delivery logistics, and customer rating distributions to optimize marketplace performance and partner merchant revenue.


---


1. Operational & Delivery Logistics Optimization
Delivery Latency Benchmarks:    With average delivery times clustering around 54 minutes across urban hubs, establish mandatory operational SLAs for dark kitchens and high-volume merchant partners to cap preparation windows.
Geospatial Routing & Dispatch:  Implement dynamic fleet allocation in high-density zones (such as Kolkata, Mumbai, and Chennai) during peak evening hours to mitigate delivery delays exceeding 60 minutes, which directly correlate with customer churn and rating degradation.




2. Merchant Pricing Strategy & Tiering
Value-Segment Penetration:  Given that the median menu pricing sits at ₹300 with an interquartile range extending up to ₹400, platform promotions and discount structures should target the ₹200–₹350 sweet spot to maximize order frequency.
Premium Dining Onboarding:  Curate exclusive visibility packages and dine-in partnership models for high-ticket establishments (₹800 average pricing) to capture affluent urban demographics without cannibalizing mid-tier volume.





3. Quality Assurance & Merchant Rating Governance
Underperforming Partner Support: Institute an automated early-warning threshold for restaurants maintaining average ratings below 3.2 stars or accumulating high cancellation ratios. Provide operational consulting or temporary visibility suppression until kitchen standards improve.
 Top-Performer Amplification:    Fast-track top-rated merchants (4.4 stars) into curated banner placements and promotional carousels to drive conversion velocity and overall platform trust.

## **4. Culinary Trend Alignment & Assortment Expansion**
* **Cuisine Gap Analysis**: While North Indian, Chinese, and Fast Food dominate listing counts, leverage regional demand signals to introduce hyper-local or specialized culinary categories (e.g., healthy bowls, regional street food) in underserved suburban micro-markets.
* **Inventory & Menu Optimization**: Partner with mid-tier restaurants to streamline high-margin, fast-preparing menu items during peak lunch and dinner surges to reduce kitchen preparation bottlenecks.
